01 / PRIVATE

Your words stay local.

The current Mac beta transcribes with Whisper on your device. No transcript uploads, telemetry, account, or cloud fallback. Download the model once; dictate offline afterward.

02 / IN YOUR FLOW

A shortcut that feels like yours.

Choose Fn, a preset, or a custom key combination. A compact recording capsule and live mic meter keep you oriented. Escape cancels when you change your mind.

03 / JUST ENOUGH

Useful, without the noise.

Optional listening sounds, local usage totals, and last-transcript copy. The Mac beta checks the original app and field before submitting Paste; if it cannot insert safely, it copies instead.

These features describe the implemented Mac beta, not promised Windows or Linux functionality. Transcription can make mistakes; always review important text.

Full installation guide ↗

  1. Make a little space.

    Move HandsFree to Applications. Review the beta’s signing limitations, then explicitly allow Microphone, Accessibility, and Input Monitoring.

  2. Bring the model home.

    Choose Download in Settings for the English Whisper model, about 148 MB from Hugging Face. This one-time step needs internet; everyday dictation does not.

  3. Find a field. Say a thought.

    Choose your shortcut, focus a text field, tap to speak, and tap again to transcribe. Check the result. If insertion is unavailable, paste the copied text yourself.
