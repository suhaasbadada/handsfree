# HandsFree v0.1.0-beta.1

**Prerelease · Beta testing.** First Apple Silicon macOS beta. Internal app version: **0.1.0**.

**Experimental, free Mac beta.** Asset: `HandsFree-0.1.0-beta.1-macos-arm64.dmg`. [Installation and first run](../../README.md#install). [Distribution decision and upstream caveat](../RELEASING.md). No paid account is needed.

## Included

- English-only on-device Whisper dictation with Metal acceleration.
- Native menu-bar app and focus-preserving recording capsule with live microphone metering.
- Fn or configurable shortcut, Escape cancellation, and optional listening sounds.
- Focus-checked Paste, automatic clipboard fallback, and manual last-transcript copy.
- Local usage totals and explicit verified model setup.
- Persisted 2–5 minute recording limits (default 2 minutes); changes apply to the next recording. Private background transcription at natural pauses; one complete result on finish.
- Short first-dictation help in Settings; drag-to-Applications DMG with an install guide and a complete `Licenses` folder (notices and dependency source copies).

## Before installing

Requires **macOS 14+ on Apple Silicon**. Intel, Windows, and Linux are unsupported. No Node.js/Rust needed. Open the DMG, drag the app to Applications, eject the image, then launch the installed copy before permission grants. Microphone, Accessibility, and Input Monitoring require explicit approval. Download the ~148 MB English model once; subsequent dictation is offline.

This beta is **not notarized**. Its local self-signed signature is **not Apple Developer ID**; macOS cannot verify its publisher. Only use the normal Privacy & Security → Open Anyway flow if you trust the download and your organization's policy permits it. Do not bypass managed security policy.

## Known limitations

Transcription is not guaranteed accurate or verbatim; fillers may be lost. Review important text. Secure Input, external Fn keyboards, custom editors, permissions, focus changes, audio-device timing, and Spaces can affect operation. Fallback copy replaces the clipboard, and clipboard services may retain text. Submitted Paste is not confirmed insertion. There is **no automatic updater**; quit and replace the app manually.

Continuous speech without a natural pause can defer background processing until you stop. Long dictations can take additional time to finish; no instant-processing guarantee is made. There is no remote speech processing or cloud fallback.

Automated checks cover logic, native policy harnesses, compilation, archive/signature integrity, and architecture. They do not establish hardware recording quality, permission behavior on another Mac, real Metal inference accuracy, clean-Mac Gatekeeper acceptance, or compatibility with each editor. Those require explicitly authorized interactive testing and are not represented as passed here.

When published, verify the DMG against the separately attached `SHA256SUMS`. A checksum detects corruption; it is not independent publisher authentication. Keep the disk image's project license, dependency notices, and source archive with redistributions.
