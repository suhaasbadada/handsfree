# HandsFree v0.1.0-beta.1

First Apple Silicon macOS beta. Internal app version: **0.1.0**.

[Download ZIP](https://github.com/suhaasbadada/handsfree/releases/download/v0.1.0-beta.1/HandsFree-0.1.0-beta.1-macos-arm64.zip) · [Installation and first run](../../README.md#install)

## Included

- English-only on-device Whisper dictation with Metal acceleration.
- Native menu-bar app and focus-preserving recording capsule with live microphone metering.
- Fn or configurable shortcut, Escape cancellation, and optional listening sounds.
- Focus-checked Paste, automatic clipboard fallback, and manual last-transcript copy.
- Local usage totals and explicit verified model setup.
- Third-party notices and dependency source copies alongside the app.

## Before installing

Requires **macOS 14+ on Apple Silicon**. Intel, Windows, and Linux are unsupported. No Node.js/Rust needed. Move the app to Applications before launch and permission grants. Microphone, Accessibility, and Input Monitoring require explicit approval. Download the ~148 MB English model once; subsequent dictation is offline.

This beta is **not notarized**. Its local self-signed signature is **not Apple Developer ID**; macOS cannot verify its publisher. Only use the normal Privacy & Security → Open Anyway flow if you trust the download and your organization's policy permits it. Do not bypass managed security policy.

## Known limitations

Transcription is not guaranteed accurate or verbatim; fillers may be lost. Review important text. Secure Input, external Fn keyboards, custom editors, permissions, focus changes, audio-device timing, and Spaces can affect operation. Fallback copy replaces the clipboard, and clipboard services may retain text. Submitted Paste is not confirmed insertion. There is **no automatic updater**; quit and replace the app manually.

Automated checks cover logic, native policy harnesses, compilation, archive/signature integrity, and architecture. They do not establish hardware recording quality, permission behavior on another Mac, real Metal inference accuracy, clean-Mac Gatekeeper acceptance, or compatibility with each editor. Those require explicitly authorized interactive testing and are not represented as passed here.

Verify the ZIP against the separately attached `SHA256SUMS`. A checksum detects corruption; it is not independent publisher authentication.
