# HandsFree v0.1.1-beta.14

**Unpublished experimental Mac beta candidate — group 4: Clear and accessible.**
Internal app version: **0.1.1**. Apple Silicon, macOS 14+; existing self-signed
identity, not notarized. This is the fourth of four sequential lean feature
releases (beta 11–14), not a combined release. Active public downloads and the
exact audited signed feed remain **beta 13** until beta 14's public audit passes.

## Candidate scope

- **Latest-offer updater bugfix:** explicit Download & restart intent refreshes
  the latest feed **before confirmation**, without downloading an archive yet.
  Consent is bound to the exact version and controller revision; stale callbacks
  cannot reuse approval. If metadata expires during confirmation, native
  validation refreshes it before acquiring the install lease or downloading.
  A changed version requires fresh review/reconfirmation, never silent
  substitution. Expired offers and obsolete errors clear; failures are not
  reported as up to date. **Check again** stays available without restarting the
  app. Opening the dialog/local status reads do not authorize automatic,
  unsolicited feed polling. Signature/version/canonical-URL/idle guards remain.
- **Keyboard and recovery:** keyboard-operable brand menu and named status
  regions; bounded status/error scrolling; actionable existing recovery routes.
  Workflow controls retain focus across pending/cancel/failure and exclusion
  removal/picker updates without stealing focus after delayed completion.
- **System appearance:** follows the system rather than adding a theme setting;
  no native forced Light theme or fixed light window background. Reduced-motion
  and runtime-contrast coverage accompany adaptive web/native presentation.
- **Stable practice:** the original beta 12 layout, focus/privacy boundaries,
  recording modes, duration/sound controls and existing data remain intact.

No new AI features, models, transcript history, snippets, extra statistics,
per-app profiles, custom theme preferences or other product bloat are added.

## Evidence and remaining gates

Controller/fake-clock/transport tests and production-DOM browser scenarios cover
latest-feed preparation, exact consent, expiry/version changes, stale responses,
keyboard menu/status/recovery, focus and appearance contracts. Command-line
AppKit tests invoke **NSButton target/actions**; they are **not AXPress acceptance**
and do not establish real VoiceOver behavior or actual window ordering.

**Final validation, exact-source CI, signing and local/draft/anonymous-public
seven-asset audits are pending.** No beta 14 artifact source SHA, CI run, published
asset hash or deployment receipt is claimed yet. Activation CI, Pages/live
website/feed byte readback, matching Homebrew publication/readback and tap CI
remain independent gates after the public audit.

Installed-app OTA replacement/restart, real VoiceOver, microphone hardware,
actual OS login/ServiceManagement and separate-Mac acceptance are **unverified**.
Tests are not those acceptance gates. Preserve existing macOS signing identity,
updater keys, permissions and model/settings/usage data. **Do not manually replace
or install the app**; the owner upgrades through the explicitly confirmed in-app
flow. No installation, launch, microphone use or totals reset is authorized by
candidate metadata preparation. Historical beta 11–13 notes/assets stay immutable;
completed publication/parity receipts are in the [release ledger](../FEATURE-RELEASES.md).
