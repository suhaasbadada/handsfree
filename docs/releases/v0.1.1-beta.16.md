Matching Homebrew cask published and remotely read back at tap commit
`f567f00cc018e146c6f857356d2d4333d8136f78`. Tap CI, website/feed deployment
and live byte checks remain independent distribution gates, not installed-app acceptance.

Published experimental Mac beta · Apple Silicon · macOS 14+
Internal app version: **0.1.1**

## Scope

- **Coherent dashboard:** labeled Overview, Practice and Settings navigation,
  clearer page headings and a warm branded workspace. Overview presents the
  configured shortcut and all-time activity; visiting it does not grant capture.
- **Dedicated private Practice page:** the existing private test flow is separate
  from Overview. Text remains ephemeral, clears when leaving the view or losing
  its active context, and context revocation remains guarded. Page title, focus
  recovery and tray routing follow the selected view without bypassing native
  authorization. Practice does not insert into another app, copy to the clipboard
  or count toward usage.
- **Grouped Settings:** General, Dictation, Appearance & sound and Setup retain
  existing controls and safeguards in a clearer hierarchy.
- **Preserved behavior:** adaptive Light/Dark/System appearance, the warm brand
  palette and prominent update footer remain. Practice text, meter, actions and
  status keep stable geometry through state changes. Usage/data semantics and
  native guards remain unchanged; no new AI features, models or transcript history.
- Beta 14's latest-offer refresh before confirmation, exact-version/revision
  reconfirmation and once-only signed updater remain unchanged. Opening a page
  or reading cached update status does not authorize unsolicited feed polling,
  automatic download or installation.

## Honest limitations and preservation

Experimental, self-signed and not notarized. Runtime VoiceOver/AXPress,
microphone hardware, separate-Mac QA and actual OTA replacement/restart remain
**unverified**. Source tests and browser smoke are not evidence of these gates.
No installed-app replacement, microphone acceptance or successful OTA is claimed.

**Do not manually replace the installed app.** The owner upgrades through the app.
Preserve the existing signing identity, updater keys, permissions, downloaded
models, settings and usage statistics; no data reset or key rotation. Windows
remains paused and Linux unsupported; this release is Mac-only.
