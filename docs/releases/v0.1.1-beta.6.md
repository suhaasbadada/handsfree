## In-app practice

Overview adds **Try your voice** with a temporary editable practice field. After
permissions and English-model setup, choose **Start practice**, speak, then
**Finish**. This is an explicit-button practice flow, **not a shortcut test**.
To test real insertion, leave practice and use the normal shortcut in a supported
non-secure field in another app. See [practice safeguards](../practice.md).

Practice does not copy to the clipboard, paste into another app, update the last
normal transcript, save recordings or text, or count toward usage. Cancel,
Clear / try again, leaving Overview, hiding/closing the window, and losing focus
clear practice; late results are discarded. It does not bypass setup or normal
delivery safety guards.

Optional **Settings → Compatibility paste** remains **off by default** for normal
dictation. The exact field cannot be verified on that path; there is no automatic
retry and no Enter is sent. Practice never invokes it. See
[Compatibility paste limitations](../COMPATIBILITY-PASTE.md).
