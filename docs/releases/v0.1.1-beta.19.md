## Session-owned audio

- One authorized session owns a single input engine plus an optional output-only
  cue engine, held through start cue, capture and end cue, then retired. Cue-period
  PCM is discarded; there is never an idle microphone session.
- A distinct pre-accept configuration reconciliation is bounded to at most two
  attempts on the same engine, only for initial configuration change after healthy
  first cleanup. It preserves the original device UID, epoch and start gate.
  Cancellation is permanent and the global three-second startup watchdog is not
  restarted. No fallback device or global rate writes are introduced.
- Complete EOS producer drain and full retirement use separate owner barriers;
  AppKit never waits for hardware. An accepted fatal capture failure terminates
  independently of physical shortcut release; an end-cue failure is nonfatal.
- Practice status and the native capsule distinguish actual **Starting** from
  **Listening**, rather than presenting cue/setup time as accepted capture.

## Focused Practice and Settings

Practice and Settings replace Overview. Practice links to the real Dictation
settings; Settings owns the guide. The compact update indicator is 30 px with a
single-line footer. Normal Practice content needs no scrolling at 980×820 or
560×650; long content retains an accessible scrolling fallback. Saved
Light/Dark/System appearance is unchanged. No new cue asset/profile bytes or
volume-default changes are included.

## Evidence and remaining risk

Primary-reported native Joffrey rechecks found no findings. Full UI smoke checks
passed six appearance combinations, normal/minimum/tiny sizes, keyboard/context
flows and update states. Initial native Bob checks reported 24 passed; Rust
reported 90 passed / 3 ignored, with formatting and Clippy passing. These are
review, synthetic and browser evidence, not hardware acceptance of the final app.

**Full hardware acceptance has not passed.** Physical Bluetooth tests exposed the
old duplex topology and then an initial configuration failure. Fixes were reviewed
and synthetic checks passed. The final negotiation hardware attempt was blocked
by a microphone-permission timeout with **zero sessions**. The owner explicitly
waived further hardware testing, requested publication, and will update and test
the installed app personally. This does not establish audible cues, real Fn
latency, device compatibility, real OTA replacement/restart, or overall stability.
