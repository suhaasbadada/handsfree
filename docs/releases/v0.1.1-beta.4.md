# HandsFree v0.1.1-beta.4

**Planned prerelease · Beta testing.** Apple Silicon macOS candidate, not yet published. Internal app version: **0.1.1**.

## Optional push to talk

Choose Settings → **Recording mode → Push to talk**. Hold Fn / Globe (or your
existing custom shortcut), speak, and release to finish. Long holds are supported;
custom combinations finish when the first required component is released.
**Toggle remains the default**, including upgrades; no automatic opt-in.

Other keys, mouse actions during a hold, and observed focus changes cancel rather
than silently continuing or pasting into a different target. Focus surveillance
continues through processing and delivery. Escape and native HUD Finish/Cancel
remain available. Cancellation invalidates the accepted session generation;
repeats and a still-held shortcut cannot restart a cancelled recording.
See [behavior and safeguards](../PUSH-TO-TALK.md), including focus-observation
limitations and the manual hardware acceptance checklist.

Existing shortcuts, Clean/Warm sounds, volume, recording limits, model files,
settings and aggregate statistics remain in place. Updates do not reset totals
or require a new model. Fn and custom bindings remain available; clipboard
fallback, target checks and permission requirements are unchanged. A missing
Accessibility/focus target or manual-paste fallback is not repaired by this mode.

Once verified and published, quit HandsFree and replace the Applications copy
using `HandsFree-0.1.1-beta.4-macos-arm64.dmg`. The distribution label changes;
the embedded app version remains 0.1.1. No automatic updater or permission reset.

Requires **macOS 14+ and Apple Silicon**. **Experimental, self-signed and not
notarized**; not Developer ID signed or Apple approved. Follow [installation
guidance](../INSTALL.md) and managed security policy. Notices and corresponding
dependency source accompany the DMG. Verify attached `SHA256SUMS` after publication.

Automated tests and artifact audits do not establish live microphone quality,
global-hook timing, clean-Mac installation, editor insertion or separate-Mac
hardware acceptance. Those manual cases remain pending. Review important output;
clipboard fallback replaces clipboard contents. No private recordings, models,
settings or signing material are distributed.
