# HandsFree v0.1.3 — stable Mac release

Published source: `8e79b49f64ce02cc8635f99859604d7eef340ef2`.
[Exact-source Mac CI](https://github.com/suhaasbadada/handsfree/actions/runs/44202980328)
and anonymous public asset verification passed. DMG SHA-256:
`3597febe5dba10e753a0b3b31175502f1ed4c2cd9ff78996c187e052fed2ab7d`.
The signed candidate passed capture, finish and cancellation on the selected
Bluetooth route and the built-in microphone. Private dictation initially failed
on Bluetooth and with the short built-in playback fixture; a longer fixture
passed on built-in audio by button and synthetic Fn, with usage/settings and
clipboard unchanged. Physical Fn, other Macs and general Bluetooth recognition
are not verified. Tap parity and the user-operated installed update are separate
rollout gates.

## Candidate preparation

Internal app version: **0.1.3**.

The footer divider now lines up with both edges of the unchanged header
divider. This is the only user-facing change.

Requires Apple Silicon and macOS 14 or later. The candidate uses the existing
self-signed identity and is not notarized. Publication and activation follow
signed-candidate acceptance and the public artifact audit. Until then the
website and in-app feed stay on v0.1.2. Homebrew is verified separately against
the audited public DMG. The installed update is left to the user.
