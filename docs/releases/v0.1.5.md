# HandsFree v0.1.5 — stable Mac candidate

Internal app version: **0.1.5**.

The footer updater stays icon-only. When an update is available, the refresh icon
becomes a download arrow without moving or resizing. The target version is in the
tooltip and accessible label. Clicking the arrow installs and reopens HandsFree
without another dialog. The footer has no reserved update gap.

Apple Silicon and macOS 14 or later. Existing self-signed identity; not notarized.
v0.1.4 remains active until signed acceptance, publication and public verification.
