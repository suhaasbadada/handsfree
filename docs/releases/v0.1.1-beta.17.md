# HandsFree v0.1.1-beta.17

Matching Homebrew cask published and remotely read back at tap commit
`355288a924f742fb96e25b01b6acb0f4f604502b`. Tap CI, website/feed deployment
and live byte checks are independent distribution gates, not hardware acceptance.

Published experimental Mac beta · Apple Silicon · macOS 14+
Internal app version: **0.1.1**

## Crash evidence and release scope

Three installed-app SIGABRT reports from November 12, 2026 point to
`AudioCapture.start`: AVFAudio rejected an input tap format mismatch, with
hardware at 24 kHz and the client at 48 kHz after the start cue on Bluetooth.
This release addresses that observed failure path; it does not establish that
the affected Bluetooth hardware is fixed.

- Capture reads a fresh post-cue hardware format and installs the tap with a nil
  format for native negotiation. The selected device UID is validated; failure
  is an error, not fallback to another microphone.
- An Objective-C exception boundary covers capture format reads, tap installation,
  prepare/start, stop and tap removal, converting NSExceptions into errors rather
  than allowing them to abort through Swift.
- The configuration observer is registered before startup. Locked invalidation
  guards startup and PCM delivery remains disabled until capture is accepted.
- Partial-start failure attempts stop and tap removal independently, drains
  callbacks and reports a generation-bound error. There are no automatic retries
  or silent device switches.
- Privacy, authorization and usage semantics remain unchanged. No new AI scope,
  models, transcript history or permissions are introduced.

## Source validation evidence and limits

Before publication, full Node validation reported **307 passed,
1 skipped**, Rust tests **90 passed, 3 ignored**, and passing frontend build,
formatting and Clippy. Joffrey's independent native review reported **4 targeted
tests passed, no findings**.

The native checks exercise a synthetic 24 kHz converter, a 48-to-24 kHz format
change and real thrown Objective-C exceptions. They do not instantiate
AVAudioEngine, access a microphone or launch the installed app. These are source
and synthetic regression checks, not real Bluetooth/macOS 14 hardware acceptance.
Actual Bluetooth capture, separate-Mac QA and installed OTA replacement/restart
remain **unverified**. No successful hardware fix or OTA is claimed.
