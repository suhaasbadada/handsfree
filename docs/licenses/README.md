# Upstream license supplements

Reviewed 2026-10-30. These are verbatim upstream documents, not new grants by
HandsFree and not a certification of complete contributor attribution.

- `objc2-LICENSE.md`: historical workspace document at
  `d7d2fa23ceaa5e6096c923b081040e5d81b3b9df`, retained unchanged.
- `objc2-LICENSE-MIT.txt`, `objc2-LICENSE-APACHE.txt`, `objc2-LICENSE-ZLIB.txt`:
  https://github.com/madsmtm/objc2/commit/ee9a7ada2131f5944b8750428e265c15632f2a19
  (2026-03-18), raw files at
  `https://raw.githubusercontent.com/madsmtm/objc2/ee9a7ada2131f5944b8750428e265c15632f2a19/LICENSE-{MIT,APACHE,ZLIB}.txt`.
  Their original copyright wording is preserved, not invented or backdated.
- `alloc-stdlib-LICENSE`: workspace BSD text from
  https://raw.githubusercontent.com/dropbox/rust-alloc-no-stdlib/0a81fd6928ea3b33c8cd484aa4575d50ffb98012/LICENSE

The maintainer confirmed the older crates' declared license fields in
https://github.com/madsmtm/objc2/issues/826#issuecomment-6103078978 .
Issue #826 is closed after adding these texts. Issue #23 concerns alternative
relicensing, not whether the existing MIT option is available. The maintainer
also noted other contributors:
https://github.com/madsmtm/objc2/issues/826#issuecomment-6107377999 .
These supplements therefore do not certify notice completeness. Preserve
the original hashed registry sources, authors metadata, inline notices and
historical workspace caveat alongside them. The historical Apple-SDK-derived
bindings uncertainty remains documented; it is not a specific prohibition
or legal clearance. See ../RELEASING.md and ../release-readiness.md for the
experimental distribution decision and remaining testing limitations.
